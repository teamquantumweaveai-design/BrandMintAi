FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers before you automate.

What is WhatsApp automation for business?+

It is a set of approved message flows, integrations and optional AI assistance that helps businesses handle enquiries, collect information, follow up appropriately and route conversations.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business Platform (API)?+

It depends on your use case and volume. Advanced multi-user integrations and certain automated messaging workflows may require WhatsApp Business Platform access through an eligible setup.

Can you connect WhatsApp with my CRM or Google Sheets?+

Often, yes, subject to your current software, available APIs, permissions and data handling requirements. We assess compatibility during discovery.

Can automation send promotional messages to everyone?+

No. Businesses must follow Meta’s current WhatsApp policies, including applicable opt-in, template and customer-service window requirements. We design workflows around those rules.

How much does WhatsApp automation cost in Bangalore?+

Pricing depends on conversation volume, message types, integrations, AI requirements and ongoing support. Platform and messaging charges may be separate from implementation fees. Request a scoped proposal.

Can a person take over a chatbot conversation?+

Yes. Where supported by the selected platform, we can design handoff rules so a team member handles sensitive, complex or high-value enquiries.
