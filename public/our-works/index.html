Vishak B. Kashyap

FOUNDER & CEO

LinkedIn

A business builder.
From the ground up.

A second-generation entrepreneur with roots in sales, distribution and manufacturing. His family brings over four decades of sales industry experience.

From growing a distribution business to founding Custom Teamwear, Vishak brings a practical understanding of customers, operations and business building to BrandMint AI.

His current work connects digital marketing with business operations. He builds Meta lead generation campaigns, click-to-WhatsApp journeys, and automated follow-up using AI agents and workflow automation.

Explore Vishak’s journey +

Working closely with his father, Vishak helped grow Anagha Fashions, which became a Lux Industries distributor in North Bangalore approximately a decade ago.

He subsequently founded Custom Teamwear, delivering customized T-shirts, hoodies, corporate and institutional uniforms, and gifting solutions to IT companies, hospitals, industries and educational institutions.

Custom Teamwear is his separate, independent business.

CO-FOUNDER

A storyteller.
With a business perspective.

A filmmaker, digital marketing strategist and entrepreneur with 25+ years of experience across storytelling, brand communication and business mentoring.

Shreedhar connects creative thinking with commercial understanding, bringing an audience-first perspective to BrandMint AI’s strategy and practical AI solutions.

Explore Shreedhar’s experience +

His professional journey spans journalism, event management, advertising and brand consulting before moving into Kannada cinema.

His directing credits include Karanji (2009), Lift Man and Jaali Baaru Mattu Poli Hudugaru. This is part of his individual professional background, bringing experience in narrative, audiences and creative execution to the founding team.

Through creator education and business mentoring, he connects storytelling with entrepreneurship: understanding audiences, digital distribution and the long-term value of content.

At Quantum Weave, he serves as Chief Mentor and Creative & Growth Strategist. His work includes creative direction, learning design, and AI-assisted systems.
