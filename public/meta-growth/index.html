Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 7-day trial really free?

Yes. We'll create 2 posts and 1 short Reel for your business using the photos and videos you share. You don't need to pay or provide card details. The trial is available once per business.

Will you post on Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. We prepare and publish approved content on both platforms. You'll need to give us the required account access.

Do I need to provide photos and videos?

Yes. Share your logo, business photos, product images or videos. We'll use them to create content that suits your business. Onsite photography and video shoots are not included.

Are Meta ads included in the monthly plans?

Yes. Our paid plans include basic Meta ad setup and management within the limits of your chosen plan. The money spent on the ads is separate.

Do I have to pay extra for advertising?

Yes. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Meta, separately from our monthly service charge. We can help you decide on a starting budget.

Will I get customers or sales?

Our service is designed to improve your online presence and create more opportunities for enquiries. Actual results depend on your business, offers, location, competition, advertising budget and customer response. We cannot guarantee sales or leads.

What happens after the free trial?

You can choose a monthly plan if you like our work. There is no automatic payment or obligation to continue.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contract is required for the standard monthly plans. We send a payment link for subsequent months, without automatic debit.

How do I pay?

You can pay your first month's service fee securely through Razorpay using supported payment methods. Advertising spend is paid separately.
