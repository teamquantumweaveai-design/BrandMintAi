Questions businesses ask

Is ad spend included?

No. Media spend is separate. These packages cover your organic Instagram and Facebook content; paid campaigns can be discussed separately, with the ad budget paid by you.

Do you guarantee leads?

No. Results depend on the offer, audience, creative quality, market, response speed and other business factors. The system is designed to improve consistency and create better conditions for conversations and enquiries.

Can you manage only Instagram?

The core packages are designed around Instagram + Facebook. Custom scopes can be discussed for businesses with different channel priorities.

Is this an automatic subscription?

No. You pay for the first month here, and before each new month we send you a Razorpay payment link. Nothing is charged automatically, and you can stop or change your plan before the next month starts.

What happens after I pay?

You get a payment confirmation and a WhatsApp button to start onboarding. We go through your business, offers and important dates, then share your first content calendar for approval before anything is published.

Can you create the content from scratch?

Yes, the monthly system includes content planning and creation. Client inputs such as product information, offers, photos, testimonials or access to required assets may be requested where needed.
