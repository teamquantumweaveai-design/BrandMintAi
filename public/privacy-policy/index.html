Who we are

BrandMint AI Pvt. Ltd. (“BrandMint AI”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides AI, automation, business growth and learning services. This policy describes personal information handled through brandmintai.io, our direct enquiries and the services described here.

We determine how information from our website and direct enquiries is used. When we process information for a business client, the client’s instructions, privacy notice and service agreement may also apply.

Information we collect

  • Enquiries and consultations: your name, email, phone number, company, project needs and other details you choose to enter or share.
  • Learning and service requests: information needed to discuss enrolment, deliver agreed services and provide support.
  • Messages and subscriptions: communications you send by email or WhatsApp and email addresses submitted for newsletter updates.
  • AI interactions: questions, messages and information shared with our assistants. Voice features process microphone audio only when you choose to start them and allow access.
  • Technical information: hosting and security systems may process connection information, such as IP addresses, request times and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. Google Analytics is loaded only after an analytics opt-in through our cookie controls.

Share only information needed for your enquiry. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, confidential client records or sensitive personal information into AI assistants.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange requested consultations, scope and deliver services, support Academy activities, send requested updates, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Where a service requires consent, we seek it separately from optional analytics consent.

Submitting an enquiry does not by itself subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Newsletter sign-ups and optional marketing choices relate to the updates you request. You can ask to stop those communications using the message’s unsubscribe option or our contact details.

AI assistants and saved preferences

Our voice assistant uses OpenAI to process audio and provide responses. Starting a voice session shares the audio and relevant context with that provider. Captions are displayed in your tab. Contact, email or booking actions have their own review and confirmation steps.

If you explicitly choose to save voice preferences, the website stores them in your browser for up to 30 days. They may include the details you select, such as your name, email, company, interests, language and time zone. You can use the assistant’s forget option to remove saved preferences.

Service records and anonymous voice-quality counts may be processed separately from Google Analytics. Turning off analytics does not delete an enquiry, end a requested AI session or withdraw an independently approved booking or communication.

Service providers and external links

Relevant providers may receive information needed for a feature you use:

  • Hosting and data services: Hostinger delivers the website; Supabase supports contact and newsletter workflows. Some forms use our server endpoints or connected workflow services.
  • OpenAI: processes voice assistant interactions when you start a session.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: receives information when you open WhatsApp and send a message. Some website actions prepare a draft for you to review and send.
  • Scheduling: Calendly, Google Calendar or other displayed booking providers process the details needed for the booking flow you choose.
  • Google: Google Analytics receives visit information only after analytics consent. Google Fonts is requested by some pages when they load; this is separate from analytics.

External links and visitor-initiated video players, such as YouTube, can contact third-party services. Their privacy notices apply to their own processing. Providers may process information outside India, subject to the arrangements and law applicable to the service.

We do not sell or rent personal information to data brokers. We may disclose information when necessary for service delivery, a legal obligation or protection against misuse.

Provider notices: Google, OpenAI, WhatsApp, Supabase and Calendly.

Cookies and browser storage

Our cookie controls let you accept or reject optional Google Analytics and change that choice later. Analytics is off until accepted. Advertising storage and advertising personalization are not enabled by these controls.

Requested features may use browser storage independently of analytics: privacy choices, saved voice preferences, enquiry or newsletter backups, session prompts and payment-flow state. Some of this storage can contain details you submitted. See the Cookie Policy for purposes, duration and how to clear it.

Security and retention

We use HTTPS for this website and take reasonable steps to restrict access to information and protect it against misuse. No website, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain enquiry and service information as needed to respond, deliver agreed work, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the record and purpose; contact us for details or to request deletion. Browser storage has separate lifetimes described in our Cookie Policy. Clearing it does not remove copies already submitted to us or a service provider.

Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may request information about the personal data we process, correction, completion, deletion, withdrawal of consent or grievance redressal. Where applicable, you may also exercise a right to nominate another person to act on your behalf.

Email connect@brandmintai.io with the subject “Privacy Request”. Describe your request without sending passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We may ask for information reasonably needed to confirm your identity and locate the relevant records. We will explain any limitations, such as records that must be retained, and respond within applicable timelines.

You can change analytics preferences at any time. To stop optional email or WhatsApp updates, unsubscribe where available, reply STOP or contact us. Withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully performed or remove information required for agreed services or legal records.

Children’s information

Our enterprise website and direct business services are intended for business professionals and adult learners. Please contact us before providing information about a person under 18. Where a learning initiative involves children, the relevant institution, service agreement and any legally required parent or guardian consent must be addressed before processing their information.

If you believe a child’s information has been provided improperly, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Updates and grievance contact

We may revise this policy when our services or data practices change. The updated date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Privacy questions and grievances can be sent to the Privacy & Grievance Redressal Desk, BrandMint AI Pvt. Ltd., at the office address above, connect@brandmintai.io, or +91 99729 65677. We will acknowledge and review requests and work to resolve them within the timelines applicable to the request.
