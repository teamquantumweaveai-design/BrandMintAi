How cookies and storage work

Cookies are small values saved by a website in your browser. Local storage remains in a browser until it expires or is removed; session storage generally lasts for the current tab or browser session. Both may support site features.

This policy covers brandmintai.io. External services you open, including WhatsApp, Google, Calendly and YouTube, have their own privacy and storage practices.

Your choices

Optional Google Analytics is off by default. The Google Analytics script is not requested until you accept analytics. You can reject optional analytics, accept it, or customize your choice using the banner. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from reading the site or using requested enquiry features.

Your choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days, after which we ask again. If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies on the current page and may need to be repeated. Changing a choice on one device does not change another device or browser.

Third-party services

Some pages request Google Fonts when loading. Choosing to open a third-party page, start an AI session or play an external video can send information to that provider even when Google Analytics is rejected. Analytics consent does not grant microphone permission, newsletter consent or permission to send a WhatsApp message.

We do not load advertising pixels through our cookie controls. The Privacy Policy describes providers and the information involved in requested services.

Change or withdraw consent

Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of a page or in its footer to change your choice. Rejecting analytics disables further Google Analytics collection on this site and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies at the site root. The next page load will not load the analytics script unless you opt in again.

Withdrawal cannot retract information already sent to Google. Browser controls can remove cookies or local storage that this site cannot access. Clearing site data removes saved privacy choices and can remove saved preferences and local backups. It does not delete records already submitted to BrandMint AI or another provider.
